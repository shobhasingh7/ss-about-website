About Gita Guide

Gita Guide is a simple reading companion for anyone who wants to explore the Bhagavad Gita at their own pace. Its bundled verse collection and saved verses are available on-device, making the app offline-friendly. English renderings and reflections are concise interpretive paraphrases prepared for the app; they are not presented as a published translation.

Designed for iOS

Gita Guide is available for iPhone through the Apple App Store. Audio playback availability may depend on the selected verse and the device.
