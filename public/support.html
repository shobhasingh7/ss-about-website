Gita Guide Support

Help, frequently asked questions, and app information

Need help?

For questions, technical issues, or feedback about Gita Guide, please use the developer contact information on the Gita Guide App Store listing.

When reporting an issue, include your app version, iOS version, a brief description, and the steps to reproduce it. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?

Open Gita Guide to see the verse for the day. Use the previous and next controls to browse other days, and tap a verse to explore its Sanskrit, transliteration, English paraphrase, and reflection.

Does Gita Guide work offline?

The verse collection and saved verses are stored on your device, so you can read them offline. Audio features may depend on whether audio is available for the selected verse and on device capabilities.

How do I listen to a verse?

Use the speaker control to hear the English paraphrase read aloud. Sanskrit audio is available for selected verses; if a recording is not included for a verse, the app will let you know.

How do I save or find a verse later?

Tap the bookmark control on a verse to save it. Open the Saved tab to revisit your saved verses. Saved items are kept locally on your device.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Contact the developer using the contact information on the Gita Guide App Store listing. Include the steps to reproduce a bug, and remove personal or sensitive information from any screenshots.

Where can I learn about privacy and my data?

Review the privacy information on the Gita Guide App Store listing. For questions about privacy or data, contact the developer using the contact details provided there.

About Gita Guide

Gita Guide is a calm, offline-friendly reading companion for exploring the Bhagavad Gita. It offers 110 curated verses spanning all 18 chapters, with Sanskrit, transliteration, concise English paraphrases, and reflections. The English renderings and reflections are interpretive paraphrases prepared for the app, not a published translation.
